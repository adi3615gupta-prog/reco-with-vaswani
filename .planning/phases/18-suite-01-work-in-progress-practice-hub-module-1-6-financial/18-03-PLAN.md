# Plan 18-03: MCA 2021 Regulatory Disclosures (11 Ratios & Aging), Non-Corporate Partner Schedules, IFC Checks & Zero-Merge Excel Suite

## Goal
Implement the regulatory compliance, non-corporate layouts, audit controls, and export suite:
1. All 17 MCA 2021 Regulatory Disclosures (`R_Disc`) including 11 Financial Ratios with >25% variance analysis, CWIP aging, Intangibles aging, MSME Trade Payables aging, and Trade Receivables aging.
2. Non-Corporate Presentation Suite (Horizontal T-Format Balance Sheet & P&L, Multi-Partner Capital Accounts schedule for 2 to 50 partners).
3. 35+ Rule Internal Financial Control (IFC) Preventive Checks Dashboard (`Checks`).
4. Practice Hub integration: Wire `/fin-statements` into `src/pages/Hub.tsx` under Suite 01 (Work In Progress & Practice Hub) as Module 1.6 and update `AppShell.tsx`.
5. Zero-Merge Excel Export Suite (`Financial_Statements_Schedule_III.xlsx`) and CA Software export bridges (CompuTax, Winman, ClearTax schema).

## Tasks

- [x] **Task 1: MCA 2021 Regulatory Disclosures & 11 Ratios Engine (`src/components/finstatements/RegulatoryDisclosures.tsx`, `src/lib/mcaRatiosEngine.ts`)**
  - Implement all 11 statutory ratios with standard formulas:
    - Current Ratio, Debt-Equity Ratio, DSCR, Return on Equity (ROE), Inventory Turnover, Debtors Turnover, Creditors Turnover, Net Capital Turnover, Net Profit Ratio, ROCE, Return on Investment (ROI).
    - Auto-compute YoY variance %: $\frac{\text{CY} - \text{PY}}{\text{PY}} \times 100$.
    - Highlight ratios where $|\Delta| > 25\%$ with mandatory explanation textarea.
  - Implement statutory aging grids:
    - Trade Payables Aging: MSME vs Others, Undisputed vs Disputed, $<1$ yr, $1–2$ yr, $2–3$ yr, $>3$ yr.
    - Trade Receivables Aging: Undisputed/Disputed, Good/Doubtful, $<6$ m, $6$ m – $1$ yr, $1–2$ yr, $2–3$ yr, $>3$ yr.
    - CWIP Aging & Intangibles Under Development Aging.
  - Title deeds not in company name, Benami proceedings, Wilful Defaulter, Crypto disclosures.
