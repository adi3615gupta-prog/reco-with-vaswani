# Plan 18-02: Statements Generation (BS, P&L, AS-3 Indirect CFS), Corporate Tax & Deferred Tax Engine

## Goal
Build and verify the core calculation engines and financial report visualizers:
1. Schedule III Part I Balance Sheet generator with Note references, CY, and PY comparatives.
2. Schedule III Part II Statement of Profit and Loss generator (Revenue from operations, expenses by nature, tax expense).
3. Automated AS-3 / Ind AS 7 Indirect Method Cash Flow Statement (`CFS` & `CFS(W)`) with automatic working capital delta calculation and tax reconciliation.
4. Corporate Tax & Deferred Tax Engine (PGBP adjustments, Section 115BAA 22%, 115BAB 15%, regular 25%/30%, MAT u/s 115JB, timing differences).
5. Unit tests validating that Assets == Liabilities + Equity and CFS closing cash matches Balance Sheet Cash & Bank balances.

---

## Tasks

- [x] **Task 1: Schedule III Balance Sheet & P&L Calculation Engine (`src/lib/finStatementsEngine.ts`)**
  - Implement recursive grouping aggregator:
    - Equity & Liabilities: Shareholders' Funds (1001-1116), Non-Current Liabilities (1301-1552), Current Liabilities (1601-1902).
    - Assets: Non-Current Assets (2001-2406), Current Assets (2502-2951).
    - Profit & Loss: Revenue from Operations (3001-3004), Other Income (3101-3105), Expenses (4001-4615), Exceptional/Extraordinary items (4701, 4751), Tax expense (4801-4804).
  - Support multi-currency denomination units: `In Rs`, `Rs in '00`, `Rs in '000`, `Rs in Lacs`, `Rs in Lakhs`, `Rs in Millions`, `Rs in Crores`.
  - Auto-generate Note references and Note aggregates (`Notes_1`, `Notes_2`, `Notes_3`).

- [x] **Task 2: Automated AS-3 / Ind AS 7 Indirect Cash Flow Statement Engine (`src/lib/cfsEngine.ts`)**
  - Operating Cash Flows:
    - Net Profit After Tax $\to$ add back Depreciation (4501), Finance costs (4401), non-cash losses.
    - Subtract non-operating income (Interest 3101, Dividends 3102, Gain on investments 3103).
    - Compute working capital changes: Inventories ($\Delta 2601$), Trade Receivables ($\Delta 2701–2707$), Trade Payables ($\Delta 1701–1702$), Other Current Assets/Liabilities.
    - Compute Net Tax Paid:
      $$\text{Tax Paid} = \text{Opening Provision} + \text{P\&L Tax} - \text{Closing Provision} + \text{Opening Adv Tax} - \text{Closing Adv Tax}$$
  - Investing Cash Flows:
    - Purchase/Sale of Fixed Assets (from FAR / PPE), Purchase/Sale of Investments, Interest/Dividend received.
  - Financing Cash Flows:
    - Proceeds/Repayments of Long-Term & Short-Term Borrowings, Proceeds from Share Capital/Securities Premium, Finance Costs paid.
  - Reconciliation check: $\text{Opening Cash} + \text{Net Cash Flow} == \text{Closing Cash}$.

- [x] **Task 3: Corporate Tax Computation, MAT & Deferred Tax Engine (`src/lib/corporateTaxEngine.ts`)**
  - PGBP computation: PBT $+$ Section 37/40(a)(ia)/43B disallowances $+$ Book depreciation $-$ IT Act Section 32 depreciation.
  - Regime selection:
    - Regular Regime: 25% (Turnover < 400 Cr) or 30% + Surcharge + 4% HEC.
    - Concessional Regime: Section 115BAA (22% + 10% Surcharge + 4% HEC = 25.168%).
    - MAT u/s 115JB: 15% on adjusted book profit.
  - Deferred Tax Calculation: Timing differences between Book and Tax depreciation, Section 43B disallowances, unabsorbed depreciation.

- [x] **Task 4: High-Density Financial Reports UI (`src/components/finstatements/FinancialReports.tsx`)**
  - Sub-tab switcher: `Balance Sheet`, `Profit & Loss`, `Cash Flow Statement`, `Notes to Accounts`, `Tax & Deferred Tax`.
  - Obsidian Glass table styling with JetBrains Mono numbers, currency denomination switcher, and double accounting underlines on totals.
  - Instant balance integrity pill: Green "Balance Sheet Tallied (Diff: ₹0.00)" or Red "Imbalance Alert (Diff: ₹...)".

- [x] **Task 5: Unit Testing & Financial Math Invariants (`src/test/finStatementsCalculation.test.ts`)**
  - Verify mathematical balance: Assets == Liabilities + Equity.
  - Verify Cash Flow Statement reconciliation: Net change + Opening == Closing.
  - Verify Section 115BAA effective tax rate (25.168%) and MAT computations.
