    - Generates 31 clean CSV buffers matching the exact headers of `Section_wise_CSV_files/GSTR1/`:
      - `b2b,sez,de.csv`, `b2cl.csv`, `b2cs.csv`, `cdnr.csv`, `cdnur.csv`, `exp.csv`

    - Uses `JSZip` / `adm-zip` to bundle all non-empty section CSVs into a zip archive:
      `<Client>_GSTR1_SectionWise_CSVs_<Month>_<FY>.zip`.
