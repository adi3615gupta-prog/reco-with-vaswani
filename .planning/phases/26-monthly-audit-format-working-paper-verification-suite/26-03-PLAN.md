<objective>
Implement the strict Zero-Merge multi-tab Excel working paper generator in `src/lib/monthlyAuditExcel.ts`.

Purpose: Produce an audit-grade, 8-sheet Excel workbook (`Monthly_Audit_Working_Paper_{Client}_{Month}.xlsx`) modeled after the CA firm's `Monthly Audit Format.docx` and `Work Pending List.xlsx` that strictly eliminates cell merges (`ws['!merges'] = []`), preserving AutoFilters, sorting, and lookup formulas (`XLOOKUP`, `VLOOKUP`).

</objective>

<context>
In CA audit practices, audit working papers are reviewed and shared with senior partners, tax authorities, and client management. Standard Excel templates that use merged cells break column sorting and filtering, throwing the error: *"To do this, all the merged cells need to be the same size."*

To guarantee audit-grade analytical power:
1. Every sheet must enforce `ws['!merges'] = []`.
2. Titles and headers must be stored in single unmerged cells (e.g. Title in A1, Subtitle in A2) with appropriate row height.
3. Tables must use standard columnar grids with headers in Row 14/15, enabling Excel AutoFilter on row 14/15 without clipping.
4. Corporate styling: dark navy headers (`#1E293B`), white bold text, subtle zebra striping (`#F8FAFC`), currency formatting (`₹#,##0.00`), and risk severity highlight colors.
</context>
