<objective>
Implement the interactive Monthly Audit UI Workbench & Cross-Month Action Tracker in `src/pages/MonthlyAuditSuite.tsx` and integrate it into `src/pages/AuditModule.tsx`.

Purpose: Provide an audit-grade, high-density financial workbench that executes all 5 sections of the CA firm format (`Monthly Audit Format.docx`), tracks cross-month pending action items (`Work Pending List.xlsx`), connects to TallyPort 9000, performs direct client directory auto-saves, and triggers instant Zero-Merge Excel exports.
Output: `src/pages/MonthlyAuditSuite.tsx`, updated `src/pages/AuditModule.tsx`, and verified UI tests in `src/test/monthlyAuditUi.test.ts`.
</objective>

<context>
The CA firm audit format requires a unified, responsive interface where auditors can:
1. Select Client, FY, and Month from an interactive 12-month timeline.
2. Check live Tally connection status on Port 9000 and run a 1-click automated scan.
3. Review and refine the 5 core sections:
   - Section 1 & 2: Engagement profile, gross turnover, Books vs GST comparison, and 5 Core Registers scope.
   - Section 3: Detailed verification workings (Sales R1/3B, Purchase 2B, Journal & TDS 24Q/26Q, Bank/Loans/AIS, ROC charges CHG-1/4, Cash 40A(3), negative cash, provisions, FA ITC u/s 17(5)).
   - Section 4: Labor compliances (PF, ESIC, PT) vs deposit due dates, and categorized current observations.
   - Section 5: Cross-month pending points tracker with resolution workflows.
4. Auto-save directly into the client's structured audit folder (`gst_returns_data/<Client Name - GSTIN>/<FY>/<Month>/Audit Working/`) on Ctrl+S, and download the Zero-Merge Excel report at any time.
</context>

<threat_model>
<threat>
  <id>T-26-09</id>
  <description>Accidental data loss when switching months or clients before saving audit working papers.</description>
  <mitigation>Prompt user with unsaved changes dialog before month/client transitions and provide global Ctrl+S hotkey with auto-save to SQLite and client directory.</mitigation>
</threat>
<threat>
  <id>T-26-10</id>
  <description>UI freeze or lag when rendering hundreds of cash vouchers or journal entries in verification sub-tabs.</description>
  <mitigation>Use virtualized / paginated tables and high-density summary views with drill-down modals rather than rendering unbounded raw voucher arrays.</mitigation>
</threat>
</threat_model>

         - **Tab 4: Current Year Observations:**
           - High-density observation table with Risk Badges (Critical red, High amber, Medium yellow, Low green).
           - Add/Edit Observation modal with fields: Section, Severity, Category, Financial Exposure, Voucher Ref, Observation, Suggested Action, Management Response.
           - "Promote to Cross-Month Pending Point" button to instantly push observation to Tab 5.
         - **Tab 5: Previous Audit Pending Points:**
           - Cross-Month Action Item Tracker modeled after `Work Pending List.xlsx`.

           - Inline Status & Resolution Modal: update status (`Pending` -> `In Progress` -> `Cleared`), resolution remarks, resolved month, verifying auditor.
           - Resolution audit trail showing who cleared the point and when.
