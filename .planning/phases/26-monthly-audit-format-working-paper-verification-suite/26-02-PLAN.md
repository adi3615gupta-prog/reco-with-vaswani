<context>
The standardized CA firm audit checklist (`\\new_server\D\3. 2025-2026\DYP Infraprojects Private Limited\Monthly Audit Format.docx`) structures fieldwork into 5 core sections:
1. Basic Details (Auditor details, engagement team, duration, client profile, audit type, turnover inc. GST)
2. Financial Details (Turnover comparison Books vs GSTR-1/3B, tax output & ITC snapshot, 5 core registers vouching scope)
3. Sample Data & Verification (Sales R1/3B, Purchase 2B, Journal & DN/CN & TDS 24Q/26Q, Bank/Loans & 26AS/AIS, ROC charges CHG-1/4 & new loans, Cash 40A(3), negative cash, provisions, FA ITC u/s 17(5))
4. General Comments & Statutory Compliances (PF/ESIC/PT vs salary sheet, Sec 36(1)(va) deposit deadline, risk-categorized observations)
5. Previous Audit Pending Points (persistent tracking from `Work Pending List.xlsx`)

The engine must integrate with existing Tally XML tools (`tallyApi.ts`) and audit scrutiny algorithms (`auditEngine.ts`), providing deterministic calculations and graceful fallbacks when Tally is offline.
</context>

  <description>Inaccurate Section 36(1)(va) employee PF/ESIC deposit date check causing erroneous tax disallowance advice or missing substantial tax penalties.</description>

<threat>
  <id>T-26-05</id>
  <description>Double-counting or missing cash payments exceeding ₹10,000 when multiple cash payments to the same person on the same date are made across separate vouchers.</description>
  <mitigation>Group cash payments by (date, normalized_party_name) and evaluate aggregated single-day payouts against Section 40A(3) ₹10,000 threshold (₹35,000 for transporter ledgers).</mitigation>
</threat>
<threat>
  <id>T-26-06</id>
  <description>Unchecked Fixed Asset ITC leading to Section 17(5)(c)/(d) blocked credit claims on immovable construction or double depreciation deductions under Income Tax Act.</description>
  <mitigation>Scan fixed asset additions; flag works contract and building materials containing GST credit, asserting either 100% capitalization OR ITC claim, strictly preventing both.</mitigation>
</threat>
</threat_model>

  <action>
    1. Implement Section 2 & 3.1: Sales Reconciliation Engine:
       - `verifySalesReco(booksSales: number, gstr1Sales: number, gstr3bSales: number, taxDetails?: any): SalesRecoResult`
       - Computes `diffBooksVsR1 = booksSales - gstr1Sales`
       - Computes `diffR1Vs3b = gstr1Sales - gstr3bSales`
       - Checks DRC-01B trigger: if `(gstr1Tax - gstr3bTax) > 25000` OR `((gstr1Tax - gstr3bTax) / gstr3bTax) > 0.20`, set `drc01bFlag = true`.
       - Evaluates turnover thresholds: E-Invoicing applicability (> ₹5 Cr), Section 44AB Tax Audit (> ₹10 Cr).
    2. Implement Section 3.2: Purchase Reconciliation Engine:
       - `verifyPurchaseReco(prInvoices: any[], gstr2bInvoices: any[], auditMonthDate: string): PurchaseRecoResult`
       - Leverages matching tolerance and identifies:
         - Missing in 2B (unclaimed/unreflected ITC)
         - Missing in Books (unrecorded liabilities)
         - Value Mismatches (> ₹2)
         - Section 16(4) time-limit scrutiny: flags invoices of previous financial year booked after Nov 30 of current FY.
         - Section 17(5) blocked ITC: flags motor vehicles, food & beverages, personal expenses.
    3. Implement Section 3.3: Journal Scrutiny, DN/CN & TDS Scrutiny:
       - `verifyJournalEntries(journalVouchers: any[]): JournalScrutinyResult`
         - Scans voucher narrations and ledgers for keywords: `Suspense`, `Adjustment`, `Difference`, `Rectification`, `Write off`, `Director`, `Fixed Asset`.
       - `verifyTdsCompliances(expenses: any[], salarySheet: any, tdsEntries: any[]): TdsScrutinyResult`
         - Transporter check: verifies Transporter 194C(6) PAN declaration exists when no TDS deducted.
         - Section 194Q: flags aggregate vendor purchases exceeding ₹50 Lakhs where 0.1% TDS was omitted.
         - Section 194J: verifies 10% on professional fees vs 2% on technical services.
         - Form 24Q: reconciles total gross salary per Books with taxable salary reported in 24Q.
    4. Implement Section 3.4 & 3.5: Bank, Loans & ROC Charges Engine:
       - `verifyBankAndLoans(bankVouchers: any[], loanVouchers: any[], statements: any[]): BankAndLoanResult`
         - Flags stale cheques (uncredited deposits / unpresented cheques > 90 days from voucher date).
         - Verifies regular interest servicing and EMI schedules.
       - `verifyRocAndNewLoans(borrowingVouchers: any[], rocFilings: any[]): RocAndNewLoansResult`
         - Flags cash loans >= ₹20,000 u/s 269SS/T.
         - For secured loans: checks creation of charge in Form CHG-1 within 30 days.
         - For closed loans: checks satisfaction of charge in Form CHG-4 within 30 days.
    5. Implement Section 3.6: Cash Register & Fixed Asset ITC Engine:
       - `verifyCashCompliance(cashVouchers: any[], fixedAssetAdditions: any[]): CashComplianceResult`
         - Groups cash disbursements by `(date, party_name)`. Flags payments > ₹10,000 u/s 40A(3) (> ₹35,000 for transporter).
         - Computes running cash balance for each day; flags negative balances.
         - Checks month-end closing provisions (salaries, electricity, audit fees) and verifies opening reversals.
         - Scans Fixed Asset additions for works contracts or immovable construction materials: blocks ITC u/s 17(5)(c)/(d) and asserts either capitalization or ITC claim, preventing double-dip.
    6. Implement Section 4.1: PF, ESIC & PT Labor Compliances Engine:
       - `verifyLaborCompliances(salarySheet: any, statutoryPayments: any[]): StatutoryComplianceResult`
         - PF: Gross basic + DA vs ₹15,000 ceiling. Computes 12% employee + 12% employer (3.67% EPF + 8.33% EPS) + EDLI 0.5% + Admin 0.5%.
         - ESIC: Salary vs ₹21,000 ceiling. Computes 0.75% employee + 3.25% employer.
         - PT: Maharashtra/state slab verification.
         - Section 36(1)(va) Critical Guardrail: Checks actual deposit date against the 15th of the following month. If deposited after the 15th, flags permanent disallowance with calculated tax impact.
    7. Implement Checklist Auto-Evaluator:
       - `generateDefaultChecklists(auditId: string, verificationBundle: MonthlyAuditVerificationBundle): StandardChecklistItem[]`
         - Pre-populates the 8 standardized checklist items with verified statuses based on scan outputs.
  </action>
