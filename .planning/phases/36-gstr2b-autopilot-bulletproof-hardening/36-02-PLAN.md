Purpose: Eliminate 2–5 minute HTTP/Vite proxy timeouts, cross-month/external download contamination, premature 15s generation timeouts, silent CAPTCHA/session hangs, and orphaned Chrome PIDs while preserving Phase 4 navigation and Golden Rule guarantees.
Output: New `gstr2b/jobs.js` and hardened `gstr2b/autopilot.js` + `gstr2b_routes.js`.
</objective>

<threat>
  <id>T-36-06</id>
